podcasts.

A list of known links is available in the
[delightful farmOS](/community/delightful#references) references list.

or other community members in the [forum](https://farmOS.discourse.group),
[chat](https://app.element.io/#/room/#farmOS:matrix.org), or
[monthly call](/community/monthly-call).
