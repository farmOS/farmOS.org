# Chat

The farmOS community chat is a good place to say hi, ask questions,
brainstorm ideas, or just talk farming. Anyone is welcome to join, on
Matrix or IRC, whichever you prefer.

## Matrix

The farmOS room is [#farmOS:matrix.org]. You can join it in your
browser using [Element], or with any other Matrix client of your
choosing.

## IRC

The farmOS room is also available on IRC as #farmOS on [OFTC]. If you
don't have an IRC client on hand, the [OFTC web chat] gets you in from
the browser.

## Chat History

The complete history of the farmOS IRC channel is archived at
[irc.farmOS.org](https://irc.farmOS.org/oftc:~h~farmos).
